📸 Photo Tips
For best results

• Place the timetable on a flat surface
• Ensure good lighting — avoid shadows
• Hold your phone directly above it
• Make sure all rows & columns are visible
• JPG or PNG from phone camera works perfectly

How to use

1. Upload your timetable photo here
2. Click "Use This & Build Timetable"
3. The photo will stay visible as a sidebar reference
4. Fill in the schedule using the Build tab
5. Click Save Timetable when done
